# Prose First

LevLake communicates like a thoughtful family member rather than a dashboard.

Good software answers questions.

Great software answers the questions you didn't realize you had yet.

Whenever possible, summarize information before presenting raw data.

Instead of:

• Weather
• Boat
• Meals
• Reservations

Prefer:

"Smith's family will be at the lake this weekend. Saturday morning should offer the best boating conditions between the hours of 12p and 3pm. Dinner is nearly planned, but ice is still needed."

The supporting cards exist for details.

The prose exists for understanding.
