# Vision

# Purpose

Levenson Lake exists to simplify the experience of owning and sharing a family lake house.

It is a private coordination platform designed specifically for our family. The goal is not to digitize every aspect of the lake house—it is to eliminate unnecessary communication while helping everyone make better decisions with less effort.

The application should feel less like software and more like a trusted family notebook that always knows the current situation.

---

# The Problem

Family lake houses create a surprising amount of coordination.

Typical questions include:

- Is anyone at the lake?
- Can we come this weekend?
- Which bedrooms are available?
- Who is bringing dinner?
- Does the boat have fuel?
- Is the weather worth driving up for?
- Does the house need anything?
- Who is cleaning afterward?

Today those answers are scattered across:

- Text messages
- Phone calls
- Group chats
- Emails
- Individual memory

LevLake exists to replace uncertainty with shared awareness.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

The software should reduce work rather than create it.

Every screen should answer a question.

Every feature should remove friction.

Every interaction should save a conversation.

The application should disappear into the background of family life.

---

# Success

LevLake is successful if family members naturally check the application before asking someone else.

A successful visit to the lake should require fewer:

- phone calls
- text messages
- reminders
- assumptions
- misunderstandings

The software succeeds when people forget they are using software.

---

# Intended Users

LevLake is designed for a very small group of people.

Current users include:

- Kent
- Dale
- Smith's Family
- LeAnna's Family
- KJ

Unlike commercial software, LevLake does not attempt to support every possible user.

The software should be optimized specifically for how our family uses the lake house.

---

# Design Principles

LevLake values:

- Simplicity over complexity
- Information over decoration
- Calm over excitement
- Clarity over cleverness
- Utility over features
- Family over flexibility

Every feature should earn its place.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Over time LevLake should become the central source of truth for the lake house.

Potential capabilities include:

- Reservations
- Weekend planning
- Lake Brief
- Weather
- Boat readiness
- House readiness
- Cleaning coordination
- Meal planning
- Shopping lists
- Family announcements
- Checklists
- Historical lake activity

These should evolve naturally as real needs arise rather than because they are common features in other software.

---

# Decision Filter

Before adding any feature, ask:

Does this reduce friction for the family?

If the answer is no, the feature probably does not belong.

---

# AI Philosophy

LevLake is intentionally designed to be understandable by both humans and AI assistants.

The project should remain:

- well documented
- predictable
- readable
- maintainable
- easy to extend

Future contributors—human or AI—should understand not only how the software works, but why it was built this way.

---

# What LevLake Is

LevLake is:

- A family coordination platform.
- A planning tool.
- A shared source of truth.
- A lake house companion.
- A lightweight operating system for our family's lake house.

---

# What LevLake Is Not

LevLake is not:

- Airbnb
- VRBO
- Facebook
- Slack
- Discord
- Google Calendar
- Home Assistant
- A smart home dashboard
- A property management system

Those products solve different problems.

LevLake solves one very specific problem:

Helping one family enjoy their lake house with less coordination and more time together.
