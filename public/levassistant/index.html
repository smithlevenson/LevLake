LevAssistant

A family display that helps everyone see what’s coming up and take the next step.

LevAssistant is a personal household project maintained by Smith Levenson. It brings a daily calendar and afternoon planning tools to a touchscreen at home.

Google Calendar connection

When the household account owner connects Google Calendar, LevAssistant reads the primary calendar to display today’s events. Access is read-only: it does not create, change, or delete events.

Google handles sign-in and consent. LevAssistant stores the resulting authorization on the home appliance so the display can keep its calendar up to date.

For the family

The project is being developed for one household. Planning cards and sample activities are prototypes; the calendar remains the source of truth for scheduled events.

This public site provides app information and policies. The household display runs separately on the home network.
