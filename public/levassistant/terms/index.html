These terms cover use of LevAssistant, a personal household project maintained by Smith Levenson.

Purpose and availability

LevAssistant helps the household view its schedule and plan daily activities. It is under development and provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted service or complete and current information.

Check important appointments in the source calendar. LevAssistant is not an emergency alerting or safety monitoring service.

Authorized use

Use the application only with permission from the household administrator. Connect only a Google account you own or are authorized to use. The account owner controls Google consent and can revoke access at any time.

The current Google Calendar integration reads calendar information; it does not change calendar events. Google services remain subject to Google’s own terms.

Planning features

Sample schedules, planning suggestions, checklists, and rewards may be prototypes. Household adults decide which activities and arrangements are appropriate. Sample content does not create commitments or entitlements.

Privacy

The privacy policy explains calendar access, local storage, household visibility, and disconnection.

Changes and ending use

Features may change or be discontinued. You may stop using LevAssistant and revoke its Google access at any time. Material changes to these terms will be reflected here with an updated date.
